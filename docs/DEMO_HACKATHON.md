# Three-minute hackathon demo: deadline changes

Use fictional or permitted recordings and a fresh profile. Keep the UI English.
The baseline recording app, GPA, habits, scheduling and timer existed before the
hackathon. Clearly identify the new evidence-review workflow and versioned
deadline changes rather than presenting the entire application as new.

## Storyboard

- **0:00–0:15** — “A deadline can change halfway through a lecture. If I act on
  the first date, a good transcript still leaves me with the wrong plan.”
- **0:15–0:35** — Play/show the fictional Lab 2 statements: originally November
  3 at 17:00, then explicitly corrected to November 6 at 17:00.
- **0:35–1:15** — Analyze the transcript through Meta. Show old/proposed dates
  and both exact quotes in Review. Retain a readable viewport and scroll to the
  second quote deliberately on a narrow window.
- **1:15–1:45** — Confirm assignment/date and save. Reopen and show one active
  Inbox deadline and the retained earlier version. Repeat approval cannot
  produce another active deadline.
- **1:45–2:10** — Today now offers “Work on Lab 2” in a free slot. Add it and
  start the existing local timer; identify this as the new link to existing tools.
- **2:10–2:35** — Show an unresolved date requiring manual confirmation. Explain
  that transcript evidence is not a guarantee of interpretation.
- **2:35–3:00** — Actual measured results and clear baseline/new-work statement;
  public repository, runnable build and known limits.

## Ready-to-use closing copy

> claims visible, and changes the local deadline only after I confirm. Confirmed
> obligations connect to the daily plan and study timer. The demo uses authored
> examples; classroom and language-quality validation are still in progress.
